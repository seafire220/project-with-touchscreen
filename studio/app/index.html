Character Studio

Make a character from a picture, then send it to your device.

First, the part-finding model

The studio finds a character's hair, face, eyes, arms and clothes with a model that runs on this computer. It is downloaded once from its authors' pages (about 1.3 GB).

PyTorch is not installed in the studio's Python. Close the studio and start it with studio/start.sh, which installs it, then come back here.

New character

Drop a picture here, or click to choose one

Which pictures work best
  • One character, the whole body or at least head and shoulders, facing you or turned a little.
  • A transparent or plain one-colour background.
  • Eyes open, mouth closed or a little open; arms hanging at the sides if you want them to move.
  • At least 800 pixels tall. Use only pictures you have the right to use.
