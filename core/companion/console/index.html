            About this device

            The character is drawn with Inox2D (BSD-2-Clause) or Ayagami (MIT or Apache-2.0). Speech recognition on the device uses Moonshine (MIT) through sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0). The free on-device voice is a Piper voice trained on LibriTTS-R (CC BY 4.0; Koizumi et al., 2023), with phonemes from espeak-ng (GPL-3.0). The full list is in THIRD_PARTY.md in the device's software.
